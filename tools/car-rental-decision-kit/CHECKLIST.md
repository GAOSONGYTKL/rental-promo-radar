# Rental quote and deposit evidence checklist

## Before comparing quotes

- [ ] Record the original booking's total, currency and scope.
- [ ] Read the actual cancellation terms at the intended cancellation time.
- [ ] Confirm whether the original total is refundable apart from the known fee.
- [ ] Match replacement dates, stations, vehicle class, mileage and extras.
- [ ] Record the full replacement total rather than a headline daily rate.
- [ ] Record every additional permanent switching cost.
- [ ] Leave unknown amounts unresolved.

## Funding is a separate question

- [ ] Record what must be paid for the replacement before the original refund arrives.
- [ ] Record additional temporary holds separately from permanent costs.
- [ ] Record any other payments due before the refund; avoid double-counting withheld fees.
- [ ] Check available funds without treating a pending refund as posted money.

## Evidence and reconciliation

- [ ] Distinguish a search result from a confirmed reservation.
- [ ] Retain the cancellation acknowledgement privately if a cancellation is made.
- [ ] Record the final bill, any retained items and the supplier's release/refund action.
- [ ] Record the bank's actual posting separately from the supplier's release.
- [ ] Keep unresolved differences open until supported by records.

These are evidence checks derived from the source guides, not statements of universal rental-company policy. No booking or cancellation is performed by this kit.
