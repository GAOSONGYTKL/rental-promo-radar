For car rental under 25 in the United States, check Hertz or Dollar first if you are 20, and widen the comparison at 21. New York and Michigan have younger-renter exceptions. Eligible AAA members aged 20-24 can check Hertz's fee-waiver route. Compare the applicable surcharge over your billable rental days before choosing; the lowest age fee alone does not identify the lowest total rental price.

Read the date and fee scope together

The table keeps a general admission rule separate from the charge for a particular age band. Choose the branch and age band first. An amount from another state cannot fill an empty price field for your trip.

Missing prices are useful information. They identify which booking forms still need a location-specific check. We have not assigned Dollar, Fox or SIXT a guessed daily fee merely to make every row look complete. An unknown amount cannot win a numerical comparison.

Enterprise, Alamo and National publish matching state figures in these pages, so the calculation grid groups those equal inputs. The CSV still keeps each company separate. Matching age fees do not establish matching base rents, payment rules or available cars.

Hertz's general daily figure and its AAA fee-waiver route remain distinct. A waiver changes one charge after its conditions are satisfied. The reviewed Hertz pages do not establish a membership purchase price, so this comparison cannot calculate whether joining solely for the trip would pay for itself.

The retrieval date belongs to our reading of the page. It is not a promise that a rate will remain unchanged until pickup. If the selected branch supplies different applicable terms, record that newer source before revising the calculation.

Read across a single row before moving between suppliers. Extending a rental changes duration; switching from a Michigan band to a New York band changes the price rule too. Mixing those movements would make a longer trip look cheaper for the wrong reason.

Keep billable rental days separate from hotel nights. Use the duration established by the provider's quote. If the booking terms use another billing treatment or a cap, the simple multiplication must be revised. The file preserves the published inputs so that revision is visible.

The calculation grid deliberately repeats each daily input next to its source. This lets a reader distinguish a transcription error from a multiplication error. Changing the duration does not require inventing another policy; changing the location may require replacing the input itself.

The lower surcharge in this matched scenario leaves a specific difference for the rest of the quote to absorb. It does not establish the winner at checkout. Either company's base rent could change the result, and no base-rental quote was obtained here.

That is why the comparison excludes a general FAQ price without the same state-and-age scope. A larger-looking saving created by crossing those boundaries would answer a different question. Unpriced suppliers remain possible options, but they cannot be placed above or below these exact inputs until their own applicable fee is available.

Check your own route before committing

  1. Select your pickup state and age band. Keep a government-duty exception separate from leisure travel. Do not choose a branch simply because another location admits younger renters.
  2. Read the linked official page and pickup conditions. Verify license tenure, accepted payment and vehicle class. Hertz's reviewed page requires a license in good standing for over a year. The Fox program reviewed here requires a U.S. license and a corporate location.
  3. Enter your actual age, location, dates and vehicle. Avis explains that an ineligible age group produces a message during the reservation flow. Inspect the fee line and billable duration before advancing to payment.
  4. Apply only a waiver you qualify for. The Hertz AAA route requires the club CDP and membership evidence, with a credit card in the renter's name. Its current AAA Deals terms also require advance reservation. Do not carry this entitlement into another company's row.
  5. Calculate the applicable fee and inspect the complete quote. Leave the base rent and other charges separate. Stop before payment if a mandatory charge remains unexplained.

Keep the pickup fallback separate

One first-person renter account describes reaching the counter and finding the preferred vehicle unavailable. The renter declined the offered alternative and later received a callback about another vehicle. This is an individual reported outcome, not a measured frequency of failed pickups.

A renter-facing pitch frames the ordinary booking process as easy. That reassurance cannot fill a missing fee field or settle the applicable payment rule. The numerical comparison therefore uses the official pages, not the pitch.

The other qualitative material suggests considering alternative rental routes when an age barrier blocks an ordinary car. Such a route needs its own vehicle and charge checks. None of its quoted numbers supplies this comparison.

Our inference is to keep an inventory fallback separate from the fee calculation. A lower calculated surcharge cannot secure a specific vehicle. The table narrows eligibility questions and identifies missing prices; it does not establish what the desk will have when you arrive.

Keep the reference usable when the quote changes

Save the dated CSV as the reference. Record any later location-specific fee separately with its source and observation date, then update the calculation for the selected trip. A missing field should remain visibly missing until that information arrives.

Do not turn an empty fee into zero through spreadsheet arithmetic. A lower-looking subtotal produced by a missing input is not evidence of a saving. The appropriate next action is to obtain the applicable charge, then compare it on the same duration and age basis.

For this review, the original contribution is the dated consolidation and the explicit calculation trail. The table does not claim new company policies or unique access to their terms. Its value is showing where the inputs match, where they cannot be compared and what still needs a quote.
