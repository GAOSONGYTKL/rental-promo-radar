Apply the Thrifty coupon to your trip

  1. Open the official offer above and expand its Terms and Conditions.
  2. Select your actual pickup and return locations and dates. Check the participating location, vehicle class and minimum duration.
  3. Open + Add a Discount. Enter 101599 in Promotional coupon (PC), then Apply.
  4. Compare an eligible Pay Later quote and its itemized total before committing. Keep unrelated discounts out.

Treat the code as an instruction to price an eligible trip, not evidence that your order received a discount. Save the selected quote with the code, payment option and itemized total visible. If the saving is missing, keep that record rather than describing the advertised offer as a completed discount. Our inspection verified the published code and input label, not a charged reservation.

A free day is not a free complete order

The word free needs a boundary around it. Put the base rental charge in one column and excluded charges in another before comparing orders. A day removed from the eligible base does not tell you the amount left to pay. Do not estimate the saving by dividing a complete quote by the number of days: the headline does not establish that calculation.

Do not infer stacking from a code field

Use the offer page and its linked terms together. A generic description of what an input can accept is not permission to combine every promotion. Record the restriction attached to the selected offer, then check for an explicit exception. If you cannot identify that exception, leave another discount out rather than combining identifiers and assuming the lower rate proves permission.

Read the linked promotional terms. They also exclude pre-negotiated or special rates, including prepaid rates.

Separate published conditions from missing details

The birthday heading does not answer every eligibility question. In the displayed campaign bullets, we did not find a birthday-proof requirement or a separate booking cutoff. Those omissions are unresolved information, not promises that proof is unnecessary or that booking remains open indefinitely. Ask Thrifty about your circumstances before relying on an unstated condition.

Keep your desired itinerary fixed during diagnosis. First record pickup station, pickup time, return time and vehicle category. Changing the car or rental length may create an eligible alternative, but it does not show that the original order qualified. Name the alternative separately so that a working quote is not confused with a repaired version of the same trip.

Compare orders before diagnosing code failure

Compare two quotes only after matching location, dates, class and optional items. Keep payment and cancellation differences beside each total. If one quote uses a coupon and another uses prepaid pricing, they are different purchase conditions; the cheaper displayed number alone cannot tell you which commitment fits your plans. Questions before payment

Where does 101599 go?

The inspected booking form labels the field Promotional coupon (PC), separate from Discount / CDP / Club code and Voucher number (IT).

Does a headline prove my order qualifies?

Stop before payment if the quote cannot be reconciled with the conditions you relied on. Keep the campaign URL, selected terms and quote together when asking for clarification. This is a proposed checking method, not a guarantee of a refund or an entitlement to a particular vehicle. A documented question is more useful than repeatedly substituting unrelated codes.
