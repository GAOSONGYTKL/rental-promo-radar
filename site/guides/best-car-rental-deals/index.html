Compare a Cheaper Replacement With the Cost of Cancelling

Should you cancel first to get the cheaper car?

So you cancel the original booking and can re-book the exact same car directly after?

That question appears in an r/TravelHacks discussion about checking an existing rental for a lower price. Another commenter replies:

Safer to rebook first and then cancel original booking.

This is practical advice from a discussion, not a supplier’s promise of inventory or free cancellation. It leaves an important calculation unfinished: what does leaving the original reservation cost?

Which?’s report by Guy Hobbs, published June 24, 2026 describes opportunities to cancel and rebook after price drops at European destinations, while stressing that cancellation conditions depend on the booking. Its reported savings are not a quote for a U.S. rental. Neither that report nor the forum makes your existing prepaid reservation refundable without a deduction.

Read the old reservation’s cancellation cost before you switch

The following is limited to Avis.com direct-prepaid reservations. The Avis U.S. Reservations FAQ, Cancellation Policy directs customers to cancel and request a refund online. Its fee summary lists $50 for cancellation more than 24 hours before pickup and $150 within 24 hours. The detailed section describes the earlier fee as applying “24 hours prior”; do not infer a precise boundary treatment from these different formulations. Check the charge shown for your reservation before confirming cancellation.

If you neither cancel before pickup nor collect the car on the rental date, Avis says the prepaid amount is not refunded. It says cancelled prepayments are credited to the same card within approximately seven business days. That is an approximate credit timeframe, not a guaranteed date when your bank balance becomes available. Agency bookings follow the agency’s cancellation process and policies.

Our comparison: calculate a saving and a cash requirement separately

A cheaper quote is a candidate, not a completed saving. Our comparison below separates the cost of switching from the money needed while a refund is pending. The distinction matters even when you have found an otherwise identical replacement.

Write O for the original total, N for the replacement total and F for the cancellation deduction that actually applies. If the original is refundable apart from F, the saving from switching is O − N − F. This is our arithmetic, not a quoted rental rate. It assumes the totals cover the same rental and no other switching cost applies.

The break-even replacement price is O − F. A replacement at that price leaves no saving; a lower price leaves a positive saving. For the published Avis fee tiers, the required price drop must exceed $50 in the earlier tier or $150 in the later tier. Moving between those tiers raises the break-even hurdle by $150 − $50 = $100, calculated from the official fees above. It does not mean the rental itself became $100 dearer.

Do not count a promised refund as money already back on the card. Equally, do not count the original prepayment twice as a permanent cost: a refund and its timing are different entries. Keep a final-cost column beside a cash-needed-now column. The former answers whether switching is cheaper; the latter answers whether you can fund the change.

Use a confirmation sequence, not a price screenshot

Read the old booking’s exit terms while you can still decide to keep it. Price a matching replacement and read its payment terms before accepting a new obligation. Only after deciding that the switch works should you confirm the replacement and cancel the original. Keep both reservation identifiers so a cancellation cannot be confused with the booking you intend to use.

If the new quote changes the return location or the included mileage, stop calling the difference a saving on the same rental. Compare the changed service explicitly. If the cancellation amount is unclear near a deadline, leave F unresolved rather than entering the most favourable fee. An unresolved deduction means the net saving is unresolved too.

Finally, compare confirmation against confirmation: a lower search result, a successful replacement reservation, a cancellation acknowledgement and a returned payment are different stages. A screenshot of the first stage cannot stand in for the others. This sequence is our synthesis of the forum’s ordering advice and the official cancellation cost; it is not a promise of vehicle availability or a refund exception.
