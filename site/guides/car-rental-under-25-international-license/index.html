Build a document record before comparing bookings

Our method starts with the actual physical license, not a country nickname or a translation vendor's promise. Record its issuing jurisdiction, printed language, writing system and expiry. Keep those fields distinct. A document can use Latin letters without being written in English; the difference matters when matching it to the wording in the comparison above.

Beside that record, identify the exact pickup station and the source that describes its requirements. Keep a national FAQ and a location-parameter page in separate fields. Neither should silently become the other's evidence. If you change stations, reopen the station-specific questions rather than carrying a favorable answer across the trip unchanged.

Then list the additional documents actually available to you: translation, IDP, passport and any requested travel record. Label each as original, copy or digital so the person reviewing your notes can see what you intend to present. The purpose is to expose a mismatch early. This worksheet does not authenticate a license or approve a translation provider.

Do not put complete document numbers or images into an ordinary planning email. Describe the document type and ask for the applicable requirement through the rental company's official contact channel. If the company needs sensitive documents, establish its authorized submission route before sending them. The downloadable record is designed for local planning and contains no field requesting a full passport or license number.

Work the uncertain case without borrowing another company's rule

Consider a traveler whose document question is still unresolved. Start with the issuing country and the text that describes acceptance; do not begin by purchasing whichever translated document has the most convincing name. Put the unresolved condition beside the intended pickup. An attractive booking price belongs elsewhere in the record and cannot answer that document question.

When one source recommends an item and another requires it, preserve both statements with their scopes. Our comparison does not choose the easier one. Ask which source applies to this station and this original license. Until that is clear, the right output is an unresolved entry, not a manufactured nationwide requirement and not a promise of acceptance.

For a group sharing the driving, use a separate record for each person rather than assuming a companion's documents cover everyone. Route the authorization question to the additional-driver guide. This page only helps organize the document question; it does not appoint an additional driver or override the rental agreement.

Finish the preparation by writing down what changed after clarification. Keep the source, date and specific question answered. If a reply only discusses translation, do not mark every other pickup condition as cleared. A document bundle and a confirmed rental arrangement are different outputs.

Questions to resolve before packing

Can the IDP replace my original license?

No. The reviewed company pages require the original license alongside any IDP. Treat a missing original as an unresolved pickup problem, not something a translated booklet automatically fixes.

Does checking the documents also clear the under-25 restrictions?

No conclusion about the other checks follows from this worksheet. Keep the age route, payment qualification, vehicle category and driver authorization separate. Use the linked guides for those questions instead of rewriting a license answer as a complete approval.

What if I cannot establish which translation is accepted?

Leave that field unconfirmed and ask the actual pickup location for the applicable requirement. Record the answer with its scope. This guide has not verified a translation seller or tested a counter's acceptance, so it does not recommend buying an unspecified document to close the gap.
