Choose a program before choosing a coupon

Avis describes Flex as a long-term option and publishes a minimum of 15 days and maximum of 330 days. Its comparison table lists unlimited mileage and included preventative maintenance. These are product descriptions, not a quote for your trip. Read the Avis long-term page.

Hertz lists a 63-day minimum for its named Multi-Month product, extendable monthly rentals and no mileage limit. It also says one-way monthly rentals carry a fee. None of those statements supplies a universal monthly rental price. Read the Hertz Multi-Month page.

Sources read October 25, 2026. Availability, eligibility and the agreement at the selected location still need confirmation. This comparison is about the two named programs; it is not an exhaustive market ranking.

Our full-stay comparison: make every quote cover the same job

Create one row for each quote. Start with exact pickup and return times, pickup and return locations, vehicle category and the named product. Keep those fields fixed while comparing suppliers. A cheaper number for a different return station or smaller car is a different proposal. Put it in a separate row rather than declaring a saving.

Next, divide the money into three columns: confirmed rental charges, optional choices you intend to buy, and money held as security. The first two can affect what you spend. A refundable hold affects available funds but should not automatically be counted as a final cost. Record what the agreement says instead of treating every card authorization as a fee.

Keep a fourth field for missing information. If a quote omits an extension rate, mileage allowance or early-return calculation, write “not confirmed.” Do not turn an empty field into zero. A comparison with visible unknowns is more useful than a complete-looking total assembled from assumptions.

Do the comparison twice if your return date is uncertain: once for the earliest plausible return, then again for the latest. Ask the supplier to explain the price for each scenario. This identifies whether the apparent saving depends on keeping the car longer than you actually need. These are proposed quote scenarios, not bookings we performed.

Convert billing periods without inventing a price

Use the actual billing periods written in the quote. Our calculation is: confirmed full-stay cost = sum of quoted period charges + separately quoted mandatory charges + selected optional charges. Add each item only once. If tax or a fee is already included, do not add it again.

For comparing different trip lengths, calculated cost per calendar day = confirmed full-stay cost ÷ the number of calendar days in that same scenario. This is our arithmetic comparison, not the supplier’s billing rule. An advertised daily rate multiplied by a guessed month length does not establish the contractual total.

Do not use this formula to fill gaps in a quotation. If the supplier has not stated what happens to a partial period, ask for the complete revised quote. Keep a note of whether an early return changes the rate. A daily equivalent can make two confirmed totals easier to compare; it cannot confirm an unquoted refund.

Month to month, short term and cheapest monthly searches

A rolling need and a fixed trip are different decisions. If you need flexibility, ask whether the next period requires approval, a new agreement, another authorization or a different price. Keep the answer beside the original return date. Do not assume the word monthly promises automatic renewal.

For a shorter stay, compare a quote for your actual dates with the named longer program only if you meet its published duration conditions. Extending the search dates merely to reveal a lower headline rate leaves you with a different trip.

To find the cheapest monthly car rental for your own plans, compare the confirmed full-stay totals on like-for-like rows. We have not collected live reservations and cannot name a cheapest supplier. A coupon is worth evaluating only against a comparable quote without it, including any benefits that disappear when it is applied.

Questions before you commit

Does monthly mean a calendar month? The label alone does not answer that. Use the billing period and return time in the actual quote; the two named products above have different published duration thresholds.

Can I call a rental cheapest from its daily rate? Not from that figure alone. Compare confirmed totals for identical dates, location and category, then show the calculated daily equivalent separately.

What if I am under 25? See our separate guide to young-driver long-term eligibility and extensions. This page does not replace that eligibility check.
