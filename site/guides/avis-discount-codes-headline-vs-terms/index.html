Avis discount codes: does the headline match the terms?

No. The Southwest Avis offer does not fully agree with itself: its headline and AWD instruction promise up to 35% off base rates, but a later condition says up to 30% on leisure weekly and weekend base rates. Neither figure guarantees your saving.

Source review: 2026-10-06. No booking test was performed.

The page reviewed on October 6, 2026 lists AWD K024798 for the rate discount and coupon MUAB189 for bonus points. It requires its booking link, an advance reservation and a Rapid Rewards account number for points. Qualifying rentals are at participating Avis airport locations in the United States and Canada and must begin by January 31, 2027. Taxes and other listed charges are extra. Other promotions cannot be combined with this offer. These conditions identify the promotion; they do not resolve the conflicting percentages.

When a cheaper booking does not prove the advertised percentage

A cheaper quote can be real while the explanation attached to it is wrong. Successful code changes and higher coded prices both appear in rental experiences; those outcomes cannot identify which percentage governed this particular offer. One apparently improving quote also involved a change of car class, so it cannot isolate the code's effect. The mismatch belongs to the advertisement; the saving belongs to a particular reservation.

When the website and rental desk disagree

A working code answers a software question; it does not settle the rental contract. An online reservation can accept an identifier while a rental agent questions eligibility. A reversed counter refusal and an expired offer are different failures. In the former case, an initial refusal was followed by acceptance after the issue was reviewed; that sequence does not prove a code had expired. An uneventful pickup elsewhere does not make the eligibility question disappear.

When yesterday’s advice describes a different benefit

Advice that a benefit has disappeared can coexist with a recent successful booking. That leaves the program's current status unresolved until its authorized terms are checked; it does not justify choosing whichever claim sounds more convenient. A work-travel rate and a personal-use benefit also need their own eligibility checks, even when both are casually called a company discount.

When the lower number may describe a different package

A question about included protection is not confirmation that protection is included. A comparison that omits the answer cannot establish that the lower quote buys the same package. That is the small detail behind a large mistake: a price disagreement may concern what is being sold, rather than a discount calculation.

For this offer, the unresolved question is specific: does AWD K024798 follow the 35% wording, the 30% leisure-rate condition, or a narrower qualification not explained by those lines? The published page does not supply that answer. Calling the offer verified would conceal the very discrepancy a renter needs to see.
