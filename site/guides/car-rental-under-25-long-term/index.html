Build an extension record, not a cheaper-looking daily rate

Our comparison method keeps the original agreement beside the proposed continuation. Give each its own return date, program name, pickup location and written price breakdown. Leave an unknown blank. A longer period in a search form does not tell you which agreement would authorize keeping the car you already have.

Next, separate a request from a response. Put the time you asked for extra days in one field and the company's confirmed return date in another. If you only have the first field, your planning record is incomplete. This is a way to organize evidence, not an assertion that every company uses an identical extension process.

For cost, compare the existing estimate with the replacement estimate line by line. Ask whether the revised total includes the age-related charge already. Do not add that line again simply because the base rate is displayed separately. If the source does not specify whether a charge continues, record that question instead of turning a monthly label into a waiver.

Keep the authorization question alongside the price question, but never treat them as the same amount. Record what the company says it must authorize and what your card provider shows as available. An inexpensive continuation can still leave a funding question unresolved. This worksheet does not calculate a universal hold or tell you when your bank will release it.

Test the program name before using its duration rule

Suppose you need the vehicle beyond the original return date. Start with the product name on the actual agreement. Then locate the duration condition attached to that product, rather than selecting the most favorable number from another part of the website. An offer panel and a general product description can describe different arrangements on the same page.

Make a separate row for any proposed offer. Record its begin-by deadline, minimum keep and applicable country before comparing the headline benefit. A deadline in the past is a reason to stop treating that offer as available; it does not establish that the company's entire long-term service has ended.

Finally, ask what changes if you return earlier than the proposed continuation date. Preserve the answer with the location and product it describes. Until the company confirms that point, neither this worksheet nor a headline monthly rate supplies an early-return price. The useful output is a dated record of the unresolved condition, not a manufactured cheapest-company ranking.

Questions before asking for extra days

Does extending automatically remove the young-renter surcharge?

The reviewed sources do not establish that. Avis explicitly retains a warning about a daily under-25 surcharge on its long-term page. No universal waiver is claimed here.

Can I use the old estimate as the price of an Enterprise extension?

Enterprise says rates may change with the extension length and that an additional authorization is needed. Ask for the revised terms through the extension route described in its FAQ rather than treating the original estimate as the continuation price.

Is the Hertz minimum the minimum for every monthly rental?

This comparison only attributes the listed minimum to the named Multi-Month program. It does not establish the terms of every Hertz booking, location or separate monthly product.

Use the adjacent checks only when they apply

Keep company eligibility, payment-card qualification, available funds and deposits and age-fee calculations on their own pages. This guide concerns the continuation of a rental, not a new approval for those separate questions.
