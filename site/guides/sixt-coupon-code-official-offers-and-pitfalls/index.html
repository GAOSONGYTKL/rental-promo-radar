If a Separate Voucher Fails

The checks below concern a separately entered voucher, not a payment requirement for the Weekday Special card above.

  • Payment: Your voucher requires Pay at Pick-up; follow the selected promotion’s own payment conditions.
  • Country: The rental country meets the voucher’s eligibility conditions.
  • Vehicle: Your selected vehicle group is included.
  • Rental period: Your trip fits the permitted rental period.
  • Expiry: SIXT’s voucher help instructions say the rental must finish before the voucher expires.
  • Existing booking: The voucher instructions exclude retroactive application to existing or past bookings.

Real-World Pitfalls to Check Before You Book

Membership or app pricing costs more

Why might the app or member quote be higher, including a reported €350 difference, when the available account of the exchange says support could not identify the cause without a booking number and would refer it to the technical team? Save both complete quotes and the account settings used, compare the same trip, and request a booking-specific explanation rather than assuming membership guarantees the cheaper price.

Damage charges are difficult to explain

How should you handle a reported €35 dispute fee or a charge for a small mirror scratch when another account describes an underside impact without a charge? Photograph the vehicle at pickup and return, retain the inspection records, and ask for the specific charge, damage evidence and applicable agreement rather than using someone else’s outcome as a price guide.

A tire problem interrupts the rental

What should you do if a tire problem leaves you driving to another branch or needing a tow, as one renter described across two rentals with no extra replacement charge? Before continuing to drive, contact the rental provider for instructions, record the fault and assistance reference, and confirm replacement arrangements and any charges instead of assuming your case will receive the same treatment.

Coverage and getting moving are different questions

Does buying full coverage answer what happens when the car cannot be driven, or where it goes after a return to a different location? Ask separately about damage coverage, roadside assistance, towing, replacement transport and return instructions, and obtain the applicable written terms before relying on any of them.

Compare the Order You Actually Need

Use your intended trip as the comparison baseline. Keep the pickup and return locations, dates, vehicle group, payment option and selected extras consistent where possible.

Record the final displayed total for each route. If a route requires a different payment option, mark that difference instead of treating the quotes as identical. A useful comparison explains both the price and what changed to obtain it.

Save three items together: the offer conditions, the booking route and the final quote. If a voucher fails, these records help isolate whether the problem concerns eligibility, application or the quote you expected.
